# Rise of Realms

**Play it here: https://alenzacodes.github.io/rise-of-realms/**

A 15-minute strategy game for friends. Lead your nation from the Ancient Age to a colony on Mars. Everyone plans in secret, then every move happens at once.

- 2 to 8 players, phone or laptop
- Join with a room code, no login or download
- Bots fill empty seats, so you can also play solo
